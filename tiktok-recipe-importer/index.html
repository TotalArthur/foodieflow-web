What is a TikTok recipe importer?

A TikTok recipe importer is a tool that reads a TikTok cooking video and turns it into a proper, structured recipe: an ingredient list with quantities, and a set of numbered steps you can actually cook from. TikTok itself has no ingredient list under the video and no method broken into steps, so without an importer, getting a recipe out of a video means pausing, rewinding, and writing it all out by hand.

FoodieFlow's TikTok recipe importer does that work for you. Paste the link, and within seconds the video becomes a recipe sitting in your library, ready to add to this week's plan and your shopping list.

How it works, in short

  • Paste the TikTok link into FoodieFlow, or share it straight into the app from TikTok on iOS.
  • Review the result. Ingredients and steps are extracted automatically, and you get to check and fix anything before it saves.
  • It's a real recipe now. Add it to your weekly plan and its ingredients land on your aisle-sorted shopping list automatically.

Three steps from video to dinner

  1. Paste the link. Copy the TikTok (or Instagram or YouTube) URL and paste it into FoodieFlow's importer. On iOS, you can also share a video straight into the app from TikTok's share sheet.
  2. Check the extracted recipe. FoodieFlow separates out the ingredients, with quantities and units, from the method, and estimates nutrition where the video or caption has enough detail. Nothing saves until you've reviewed it.
  3. Plan and shop. Slot the recipe into any day of your weekly plan, and its ingredients merge straight into your shopping list, sorted by aisle and with anything you already own left off.

Frequently asked questions

How does the FoodieFlow TikTok recipe importer work?

Paste a TikTok link into FoodieFlow, or share the video straight into the app on iOS. It reads the video and caption and pulls out the ingredients and method, then shows you the result to review before it's saved to your recipe library.

Does it work with Instagram Reels and YouTube too?

Yes. The same importer works for Instagram and YouTube links, along with any recipe URL, a photo of a cookbook page or handwritten card, and PDF or text files.

Do I have to double-check the import?

Every import shows you the result first, so you can fix an ingredient or a step before it's added to your library. Nothing saves silently in the background.

What happens to the recipe once it's imported?

It becomes a normal recipe in your FoodieFlow library, with quantities, units, and nutrition where the video or caption has it. You can slot it into your weekly plan and its ingredients flow straight onto your aisle-sorted shopping list.

Is the TikTok recipe importer free?

Yes, the free plan includes 3 video imports a week. FoodieFlow Pro raises that to 20 video imports a week, alongside unlimited meal ideas and full nutrition.

What if the TikTok video gets deleted later?

Doesn't matter. Once a video's been imported, it exists as its own recipe in your library, independent of whether the original video is later deleted or made private.
