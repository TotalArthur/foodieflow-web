FoodieFlow Legal

Privacy Policy.

How FoodieFlow collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In plain terms

FoodieFlow helps you plan meals and shop for them. To do that we store your account, the recipes you save, your plans, your shopping list and your pantry.

We never sell your data. When you use an AI feature, the content you're working on is sent to Google's Gemini AI to produce the result. We use Gemini on a paid plan, so Google does not use it to train its models.

The free version shows optional, non-personalised video adverts from Google AdMob. Pro members don't see adverts.

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

About this policy

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FoodieFlow collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when you use the FoodieFlow app on iOS and Android, and the foodieflow.app website (together, the "Service").

If you do not agree with this policy, please stop using the Service.

Who Runs FoodieFlow

FoodieFlow is built and run by one independent developer based in Scotland, United Kingdom, working as a sole trader. FoodieFlow is not a company. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean that developer.

We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy, under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. You can reach us about anything in this policy through the support form.

Information we collect

Account Information

When you create a FoodieFlow account, we collect:

  • Your email address, used to sign you in and contact you about the Service
  • Your name, as you enter it, which is shown in the app and to people in your household
  • A username, which you can sign in with and which other people can use to invite you to a household
  • Your password, which is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it ourselves

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from them. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only receive the relay address Apple gives us.

If you continue as a guest, we create an account with no email or name attached. Guest accounts and their data are deleted 30 days after they are created, unless you sign up and keep your recipes.

Preferences and Household Details

To personalise the Service, you can tell us:

  • Your dietary preferences, allergies, and ingredients you dislike
  • How many people you cook for, and whether you use metric or imperial measurements
  • Your app language
  • The supermarket you shop at and a weekly food budget, if you choose to set them
  • Your notification and shopping list settings

Allergies and some dietary choices (for example halal, kosher or gluten-free) can reveal information about your health or religious beliefs. You give us this information by choice, and we only use it to tailor your meal ideas and shopping list. You can change or remove it at any time in the app.

Content You Create

To run the Service, we store the content you create in the app, including:

  • Recipes you save to your library, along with any photos you upload, notes, favourites and custom categories
  • Meal plans, saved week templates and your meal history
  • Your shopping list
  • Your pantry, including quantities and use-by dates
  • Recipes you import from a link, video, photo or file (see "AI Features" below)

Usage and Technical Data

To keep the Service working and improve it, we collect:

  • App version, device type and operating system
  • Error logs, which are linked to your account so we can work out what went wrong
  • A device token for push notifications, if you allow notifications
  • Counts of how many AI features you have used, so we can apply the limits of your plan

If you agree to it, we also use Google Analytics for Firebase to see which screens are used and where people get stuck. Analytics is off unless you tick the box when you sign up or accept these terms. It is tied to a random account ID and your plan type (Free or Pro), never to your name or email address, and we only look at it in aggregate. Where analytics is based on your consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us.

Support and Feedback

If you send feedback from inside the app, we store your message, the topic you picked, your name and your account ID. If you use the support form on our website, we receive your name, email address and message. The form is run by Formspree, who pass it on to us by email.

How we use your information

What We Use It For, and Why

Data protection law requires us to have a legal reason (a "lawful basis") for each use of your information. Ours are:

  • Running the Service you signed up for (contract): your account, library, plans, shopping list, pantry, households, AI features, imports, photos, notifications you have asked for, and syncing across your devices
  • Allergies and dietary details (your explicit consent): used only to tailor meal ideas and filter what we suggest
  • Usage analytics (your consent): only if you opt in
  • Error logs, security and preventing abuse (legitimate interests): keeping the Service reliable and safe, and stopping misuse of free allowances
  • Adverts in the free version (legitimate interests, plus consent where Google's consent form asks for it): see "Advertising" below
  • Answering support requests and feedback (legitimate interests)
  • Keeping records we are legally required to keep (legal obligation)

Communications

We use your email address for sign-in emails (such as verifying your address or resetting your password), to reply to support requests, and to tell you about important changes to the Service or these policies. We do not send marketing emails.

Push notifications, such as reminders to plan your week, tonight's dinner, pantry items about to go off, or household invites, are only sent if you allow notifications. You can turn each type off in the app's settings, or turn them all off in your phone's settings.

AI Features

Several features use Google's Gemini AI. When you use one, we send the content needed for that feature from our server to Gemini:

  • Meal ideas (Foodie Assistant): your request, plus your dietary preferences, allergies, dislikes, household size, pantry items, budget and language
  • Importing a recipe: the web page at the link you paste, the photo or file you choose, or for YouTube and TikTok videos, the video's public title, description and transcript
  • Nutrition estimates: the recipe's ingredients and servings
  • Price estimates: the items on your shopping list and the supermarket you picked
  • Sorting your library and shopping list: recipe titles and ingredient names

We use Gemini on a paid plan. Under Google's terms for paid use, Google does not use this content to train or improve its models. Google may keep it for a short period to detect abuse, as its terms allow. Photos and files you import are used to read the recipe and are not stored by us afterwards.

To import a video, our server also asks YouTube or TikTok for the video's public details. To import from a link, our server visits that web page.

Recipe Photos

If you save a recipe without a photo, our server searches Unsplash for a matching photo using the recipe's title and your app language. Nothing else about you is sent. To keep searches down, we remember which photo matched each title and reuse it for anyone who saves a recipe with the same title. The photo is shown with a credit to the photographer.

Barcode Scanning

When you scan a barcode, the app uses your camera to read it on your device. Camera images are not saved or sent anywhere. The barcode number is then looked up directly from your device on Open Food Facts, a free public food database, which will see your IP address like any website does.

Sharing with other people

Households

If you create or join a household, the other members can see your name and the things the household shares. That includes the shared shopping list, meal plans and week templates, the shared pantry if your household uses one, and any recipes you choose to share to the household cookbook.

Anyone who knows your exact username can invite you to their household. You choose whether to accept, and you can leave at any time.

Sharing a Recipe

When you share a recipe as a link, the whole recipe is packed into the link itself. Anyone you send it to, or anyone they pass it on to, can open it on foodieflow.app and read it, without needing an account. We do not store a copy, but like any web address, the link may appear in our website host's server logs. Recipes shared as an image work like any other photo you send.

Subscriptions and payments

Payment Handling

All purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your card details, billing address or other financial information.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives your purchase records from Apple or Google, linked to your FoodieFlow account ID, and tells us whether you have Pro, whether you are in a trial, and when it renews or ends. If you redeem a voucher code, we record that you used it.

Advertising

Adverts in FoodieFlow

The free version of FoodieFlow shows adverts from Google AdMob to help cover the cost of running the Service. At present these are optional rewarded videos: short adverts you can choose to watch to unlock an extra AI generation. You never have to watch one to use the app's core features. Pro members are not shown adverts.

We only ask Google for non-personalised adverts. That means adverts are not chosen based on your interests or what you do in other apps. We do not send Google your account details, recipes, plans, shopping list, pantry, dietary preferences or allergies.

Information Used for Advertising

Even for non-personalised adverts, the Google AdMob software in the app collects some information directly from your device so it can show adverts, count them, limit how often you see the same one, and prevent fraud. This includes:

  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system and app version
  • On Android, your device's Advertising ID. On iOS, the app does not ask for permission to track you, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to it
  • Approximate location based on your IP address
  • Whether an advert was shown, watched or tapped

Google uses this information under its own Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy, and explains how it uses data from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your Advertising Choices

In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any adverts are loaded. You can review or change your choice at any time from the advertising privacy option in the app's settings.

On Android you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads.

Cookies and device storage

Cookies and Device Storage

The foodieflow.app website does not set any cookies of its own and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads. The support form is run by Formspree, as described above.

The app saves a small amount of information on your device so it works properly, such as keeping you signed in, your language and appearance settings, and your analytics choice. This is needed for the app to work and is not used for tracking. Google AdMob may also store information on your device as described in "Advertising".

Data storage and security

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored with Google Cloud and Firebase, encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is protected by sign-in and by security rules, so that only you can see your own account data, apart from what you choose to share with your household or through a recipe link.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from loss, misuse or unauthorised access. No system is completely free of risk, so please use a strong, unique password for your FoodieFlow account.

Service Providers

We use these providers to run the Service. Each one only gets the information it needs for its job:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: sign-in, database, photo storage, server functions and push notifications
  • Google Gemini API: AI features (see "AI Features")
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: usage analytics, only if you opt in
  • Google AdMob: adverts in the free version (see "Advertising")
  • RevenueCat: subscription management
  • Apple and Google: app stores, payments, and "Sign in with Apple" or "Sign in with Google" if you use them
  • Unsplash: recipe photos, using the recipe title only
  • Open Food Facts: barcode lookups
  • YouTube and TikTok: public video details when you import a video
  • Formspree: the support form on our website

Most of these act on our behalf under data processing terms. Apple, Google (for AdMob and sign-in), Unsplash, Open Food Facts, YouTube and TikTok handle the information they receive under their own privacy policies.

International Transfers

Some of our providers, including Google, RevenueCat and Formspree, store or process data outside the UK, mainly in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is protected by UK adequacy regulations (such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or by standard contractual clauses approved for use in the UK.

Data Retention

  • Your account data is kept for as long as your account is open
  • Guest accounts are deleted 30 days after they are created
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed as described under "Account Deletion"
  • Support and feedback messages are kept for as long as we need them to deal with your request
  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be kept to help improve the Service
Account deletion

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app, under Profile › Security › Delete Account. This permanently removes:

  • Your sign-in account, profile, username and preferences
  • Your recipes and uploaded photos, meal plans, templates, history, shopping list and pantry
  • Your subscription and usage records held by us, and your notification settings and device tokens

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Please cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first.

If you are in a household, please leave it before deleting your account so that anything you shared with it is removed too. If you can't use the app, or you think something has been left behind, ask us through the support form (choose "Privacy / Data Request") and we will delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

Your Rights Under UK GDPR and Applicable Law

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access: ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your personal data
  • Restriction: ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: object to us using your data based on our legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent: where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it

To use any of these rights, please contact us via the support form. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. If you live in the EU, you can also contact your local data protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to sort it out first, so please get in touch with us.

Third parties and children

We Do Not Sell Your Data

FoodieFlow does not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not work with data brokers, and we never give your recipes, plans, shopping list, pantry, dietary preferences or allergies to advertisers or marketing platforms.

We only share data as described in this policy: with the providers that help us run the Service, with your household when you choose to join one, with people you send a recipe link to, and where the law requires it.

Children's Privacy

FoodieFlow is not intended for children under 13. In some countries the minimum age for agreeing to things like analytics without a parent is higher, up to 16 in parts of the EU. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us via the support form and we will remove the account and its data promptly.

Changes and contact

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app and ask you to review them before you carry on using FoodieFlow.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns or requests about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us via the support form. We aim to respond to privacy enquiries within 48 hours on business days.
