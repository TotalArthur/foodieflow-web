How it works

From fridge to table in five steps.

Getting started takes minutes. Everything else happens automatically.

1

Set up your profile

Add your dietary preferences, allergies, and anything you want to avoid — then add the rest of your household as their own chef profiles. Takes two minutes.

2

Import your recipes

Bring in recipes you already love from TikTok, Instagram, a URL, or a photo. They'll sit in your library alongside Foodie Assistant's suggestions.

3

Generate meal ideas

Tap Generate and FoodieFlow produces personalised meal suggestions based on your preferences and what you have at home.

4

Plan your week

Drop meals into your weekly planner. Mix Foodie Assistant suggestions with meals from your personal library.

5

Shop and cook

Your shopping list is automatically generated from your plan, grouped by aisle. Walk in prepared.
