How FoodieFlow works

Save it. Plan it. Shop it. Cook it.

Four jobs that used to need four apps and a notes page. Each step feeds the next, so the week mostly runs itself.

01 · SAVE

Every recipe you love, in one place.

  • Import from any link, a TikTok or YouTube video, or a photo of a cookbook page
  • Share straight into FoodieFlow from TikTok, YouTube or your browser
  • Send a recipe to a friend as a photo card or a link
02 · PLAN

Your week, in one tap.

  • Fill My Week learns from your ratings, skips and quick nights
  • Repeat last week, or save a week as a template Pro Chef
  • Set servings per meal and the quantities scale. A Sunday nudge if next week's empty.
03 · SHOP UK BETA

Know what your shop costs before you go.

  • Pick your supermarket, set a weekly budget, and ideas aim to fit it
  • The list merges quantities and takes off what's in your pantry
  • Sorted into 13 aisles, and it updates when the plan changes
