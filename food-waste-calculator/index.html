The research

Where these numbers come from

Food waste is easy to underestimate because it is spread across dozens of small decisions a week, not one big purchase. These figures come from the most cited national studies on household food waste:

  • UK baseline: WRAP's 2022 household food waste report puts the figure at about £260 per person a year, or roughly £1,000 for a four-person household, across 4.4 million tonnes of edible food and £17.5 billion nationally. Source: WRAP, Household Food and Drink Waste in the UK 2022.
  • UK single-person adjustment: WRAP's earlier research found people living alone waste around a third more food per person than the household average, mostly fresh produce, meat and bakery not used in time. Source: WRAP, Household Food and Drink Waste: A People Focus.
  • US baseline and category breakdown: The EPA estimated in 2025 that the average consumer loses $728 a year to food bought and never eaten, close to $2,913 for a household of four, and published a full breakdown by food category. Source: U.S. EPA, Estimating the Cost of Food Waste to American Consumers.

The calculator scales those per-person averages by your household size, and adjusts for single-person households where the research supports it. It won't know exactly what is in your fridge, but it puts a real number, and a real breakdown, on a cost most households never add up.
