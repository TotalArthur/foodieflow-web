How can we help?

Common questions

Tap Generate idea on Home or in the + menu to open Foodie Assistant. Pick a mood, describe a craving or hit Surprise me, and you get a full recipe back, built around your diet and what is already in your pantry.

Tap + and choose Import. Paste a website, TikTok or YouTube link, or switch to Photo or file to snap a cookbook page or upload a PDF. A checklist shows progress, you can cancel at any time, and every import can be checked before it saves.

Open Plan, tap a day and choose a meal from your library. Drag meals between days, repeat last week or use Auto-fill for the empty days. You can rearrange or remove meals at any time.

Everything in your plan is combined into one list, sorted by aisle, with duplicate ingredients merged across the week. Items you have marked in your pantry are left off, and part-stocked ones are reduced. Change the plan and the list follows.

A household shares one meal plan, shopping list and cookbook in real time. Invite members by username so everyone keeps their own login. Creating a household needs Pro Chef, and anyone you invite joins for free. You can keep your pantry personal or share it with the household.

Free Chef covers planning, importing, full pantry tracking and the shopping list, with 10 meal ideas and 3 video imports a week and up to 28 saved recipes. Pro Chef, £3.99 a month, adds unlimited ideas with moods and cravings, full nutrition, up to 20 video imports a week, reusable week templates and household creation.

Subscriptions are managed through your App Store or Google Play account settings. From there you can upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time. Changes take effect at the end of your current billing period, and you keep Pro until then.
